Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Filevia handles information, cookies and advertising.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Filevia. Filevia provides free online tools for working with images and PDF files, including tools for converting, compressing, resizing and managing files.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Filevia website.

2. Information We Collect

Filevia is designed so that many file-processing operations can take place directly in your web browser.

When you use our browser-based file tools, the selected files are processed by the website in your browser. Filevia does not require you to create an account to use the basic file conversion tools.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card numbers or government identification numbers through our file tools.

3. Files You Upload

Filevia's browser-based tools are designed to process files locally in your browser whenever the particular tool supports client-side processing.

You should only upload files that you have the right to process and should avoid uploading sensitive or confidential information to any online service unless you are comfortable doing so.

4. Cookies

Filevia may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, analytics, security or advertising.

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled on Filevia, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve and measure advertisements.

These advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to Filevia or other websites.

5. Google Advertising

Filevia may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the website.

If Google advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to help deliver, personalize and measure advertisements.

Users may control or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and choices through Google's advertising and privacy resources.

6. Third-Party Services

Filevia may use third-party services to provide website functionality, advertising, analytics or other services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party services used by Filevia may change over time as the website develops.

7. External Links

Filevia may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites that we do not operate.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Filevia is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through our website.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its services secure. However, no website or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Filevia's services, technologies or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Filevia's privacy practices, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Filevia website.
